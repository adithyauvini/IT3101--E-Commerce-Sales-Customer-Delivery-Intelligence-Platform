# Business Insight 5 - Customer Retention

## Finding

The number of total orders is only slightly higher than the number of unique customers.

This indicates that repeat purchasing exists, but it is limited compared with the overall size of the customer base.

## Evidence

- Total Orders: **99,441**
- Unique Customers: **96,096**
- Source profiling identified **2,997** `customer_unique_id` values with more than one customer/order record.

## Recommendation

The business should strengthen customer-retention strategies such as:

- Personalised offers
- Loyalty incentives
- Post-purchase communication
- Product recommendations
- Category-based promotions

Increasing repeat purchases from existing customers may provide an important growth opportunity in addition to acquiring new customers.
