# Business Insight 1 - Sales Trend

## Finding

The monthly sales trend shows strong growth from the initial months of the dataset through 2017, followed by relatively high sales levels during much of 2018.

The final month shows a sharp decline, but this should be interpreted carefully because the dataset ends with only partial coverage for the final period.

## Evidence

- Product Sales Value: approximately **13.59M**
- Monthly sales increase substantially from late 2016 through 2017.
- Sales remain comparatively high during much of 2018.
- The final period contains limited transactions and should not be treated as a normal full month.

## Recommendation

Management should compare only complete monthly periods when evaluating growth or decline.

Promotional planning, inventory planning, and sales targets should focus on the stronger historical sales periods identified in the dashboard.

The incomplete final month should not be used alone to conclude that business performance declined.
