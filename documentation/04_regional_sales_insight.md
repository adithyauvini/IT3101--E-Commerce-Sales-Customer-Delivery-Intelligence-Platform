# Business Insight 4 - Regional Sales Concentration

## Finding

The Interactive Analysis dashboard shows a strong concentration of sales in **SP (São Paulo)**.

SP is the largest customer state by sales and is also the dominant seller state.

## Evidence

The **Sales by Customer State** and **Sales by Seller State** charts both show SP with a substantially larger sales value than the other states.

Other important customer states visible in the dashboard include:

- RJ
- MG

Their sales totals are lower than SP.

## Recommendation

The business should maintain strong logistics, seller support, and customer service capacity in São Paulo because it represents the largest concentration of activity.

Management should also evaluate expansion opportunities in other high-potential states such as RJ and MG to reduce geographic dependence.
